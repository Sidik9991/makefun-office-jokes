Laugh: Office Jokes" bounty ($5 / 10 places). 71 characters:

> I asked IT for a second monitor and got a second ticket number instead.

The joke: the thing you asked IT for comes back as the queue for
asking — a second monitor becomes a second ticket number. Six
candidates were scored against the judge's brief (genuine office joke,
turn at the end, recognition, tight wording, freshness) — see the
comments in `main.ts`; this one won on all five, and at 71 bytes it
keeps the proven short one-liner form.
